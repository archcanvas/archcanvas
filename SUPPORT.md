# Support

## Where to go

| You want to… | Go here |
|---|---|
| Ask how something works | [Discussions → Q&A](https://github.com/archcanvas/archcanvas/discussions) |
| Report something broken | [Issues → Bug report](https://github.com/archcanvas/archcanvas/issues/new/choose) |
| Report a plan that came out wrong | [Issues → Plan quality report](https://github.com/archcanvas/archcanvas/issues/new/choose) |
| Ask for a feature | [Issues → Feature request](https://github.com/archcanvas/archcanvas/issues/new/choose) |
| Show a plan you made | [Discussions → Show & tell](https://github.com/archcanvas/archcanvas/discussions) |
| Ask about your account, a payment or a refund | **Email chanmeng.dev@gmail.com** — not a public issue |
| Report a security vulnerability | [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) — privately, never a public issue |

There is also a **Send feedback** item in the account menu inside the product. It carries the context
of what you were looking at when you hit the problem, which usually makes it the fastest route for a
bug you can reproduce in the app.

## Before you ask

- The [FAQ](docs/faq.md) covers the questions that come up most: what the plans are and are not good
  for, why a generation takes a couple of minutes, how credits work, and what happens to your data.
- Search [existing issues](https://github.com/archcanvas/archcanvas/issues?q=is%3Aissue) and
  [discussions](https://github.com/archcanvas/archcanvas/discussions) — someone may have asked already.
- Check the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) before requesting a feature; it may already be on it.

## Please do not post in public

Order numbers, invoices, email addresses, passwords, share links to private projects, or anything
else you would not want indexed. Email instead — those threads are answered by the same person.

## Scope

ArchCanvas is built and maintained by one person. Support is best-effort: most things get a reply
within a day or two, and complicated ones take longer. If a thread has gone quiet for a week, a nudge
is welcome rather than rude.
